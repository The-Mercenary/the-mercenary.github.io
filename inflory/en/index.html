BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few things
to know first.

Does signing up start the service?+

This is a pre-launch application. We ask for your name, residence country/region, email, preferred platforms and monthly volume. Launch notifications are optional. We do not deliver followers, take payments or activate subscriptions at this stage.

Are these organically acquired followers?+

We are evaluating a subscription using followers supplied by third parties. Distributing delivery over 30 days does not make it organic. Genuine interest, engagement, revenue, retention, and account safety are not guaranteed. This model may conflict with platform terms and result in account restrictions; launch remains under review.

Will lost followers be replaced?+

Followers may drop, and retained quantities are not guaranteed. This survey asks whether a refill option matters to you. Its scope, duration, and terms would be defined only after we verify supplier quality and costs.

What about other social platforms?+

Start with your preferred monthly amount, then choose Instagram, a Facebook Page, YouTube, X or Other in the application. We are researching demand; availability, pricing and delivery methods are not confirmed.

About your application data

The Mercenary uses your name, residence country/region, email, selected platforms, monthly amount and consent record to manage applications and assess demand. Only opted-in applicants will receive a launch notification. Submissions are processed by FormSubmit and emailed to themercenary@duck.com (including its forwarding mailbox). This can involve processing outside your country. FormSubmit documents a 30-day submission archive. Our application retention limit is 12 months from submission, or earlier upon a deletion request; removal from the operator mailbox is handled by the operator, not automatically by this page. For access, correction, deletion or withdrawal, contact themercenary@duck.com.
