BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few things
to know first.

Does signing up start the service?+

This is a pre-launch application. We ask for your name, residence country/region, email, preferred platforms and monthly volume. Launch notifications are optional. We do not deliver followers, take payments or activate subscriptions at this stage.

Are these organically acquired followers?+

We are evaluating a subscription using followers supplied by third parties. Distributing delivery over 30 days does not make it organic. Genuine interest, engagement, revenue, retention, and account safety are not guaranteed. This model may conflict with platform terms and result in account restrictions; launch remains under review.

Will lost followers be replaced?+

Followers may drop, and retained quantities are not guaranteed. This survey asks whether a refill option matters to you. Its scope, duration, and terms would be defined only after we verify supplier quality and costs.

What about other social platforms?+

The proposed prices and quantities are for Instagram. You can select YouTube or TikTok in the interest form to express demand for future support. Pricing and availability for those platforms are not promised.
